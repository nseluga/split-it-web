Terms of Use

Last updated October 1, 2026

Split It lets one person (the host) scan a restaurant receipt and share a link, so friends can tap the items they had, see what they owe, and pay the host. By using the Split It app or opening a Split It link you agree to these terms.

What Split It does

The host's phone reads the receipt, and the host checks and corrects the items. The host shares a link. Anyone with the link opens it in a web browser, with no app and no account, picks the items they had, and sees their share of the bill, including a proportional share of tax and tip.

No accounts

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Access to a split works through its link: anyone who has the link can view the split, join it, and add or remove claims for any person on it, including for someone who has no phone. Only the host can delete the split. Share the link only with people you trust, and treat it like a shared note on the table.

Money is not handled by Split It

Split It never holds, moves, or processes money. The pay buttons open Venmo or Cash App with the amount filled in where the service allows it. The payment itself happens in that app, between you and the host, under Venmo's or Cash App's own terms. Split It is not a party to any payment and cannot refund, reverse, or dispute one.

Accuracy

Totals are calculated from the items, tax, and tip the host entered. Receipt scanning can make mistakes, and people can tap the wrong items. The host is responsible for checking the receipt, and everyone is responsible for checking their own amount before paying. Split It is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, and to the extent the law allows we are not liable for incorrect amounts, unpaid shares, or disputes between friends.

How long splits last

A split is deleted from our servers 30 days after it is created, or sooner if the host deletes it. After that its link stops working.

Acceptable use

Don't use Split It to harass anyone, to store anything other than a bill you are splitting, to break the law, or to interfere with the service. Don't type other people's personal details into names or item fields. We may remove splits that break these rules.
