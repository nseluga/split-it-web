Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. We store the split you create so your friends can open it, and delete it after 30 days.

Receipt photos

The app reads the receipt on the host's phone using the phone's own text recognition. The photo is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Only the items the host checks and then shares are stored, as described below.

What we store on our server

When a host shares a split, the following is stored:

  • the items on the bill, with their names, quantities, and prices, plus the tax and tip amounts and the title;
  • the names people type when they join (the host's name and each guest's name, including names typed in for people without a phone);
  • the host's Venmo handle and/or Cash App tag, so guests can pay them;
  • who claimed which item;
  • creation and expiry times, and a hash of a secret kept on the host's phone, which only lets the host delete the split.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, or payment card details. There is no sign-in.

Who can see it

Anyone who has a split's link can view the split, including the names and the host's Venmo or Cash App handle, and can add or remove claims. The link is a long random code that can't be guessed, but anyone you send it to can pass it on. Don't put anything in a split you wouldn't want the people at the table to see.

How long we keep it

A split is deleted 30 days after it is created, or sooner if the host deletes it in the app. Deletion removes the split, its participants, and its claims.

On your device

When you open a link and enter your name, the web page remembers that name for that split in your browser's local storage, so a refresh keeps you in place. It never leaves your device except as part of the split you joined. We use no cookies.

No tracking, no ads

Split It has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking or profiling, and we do not sell or share data.

Services that host Split It

  • Supabase hosts the database where splits are stored and delivers live updates between people on the same split.
  • GitHub Pages serves the web page, and jsDelivr (a public content delivery network) serves the open-source Supabase code library the page uses. Like any web host, these services can see your IP address and browser details when your browser loads files from them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Venmo and Cash App open only when you tap a pay button. What you share with them is governed by their terms and privacy policies, and Split It receives nothing back.
  • Apple bills subscriptions and the lifetime purchase in the iOS app. We never see your payment details.

Children

Split It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

The host can delete a split at any time from the app, and everything is removed automatically after 30 days. To ask about data in a split you can't delete yourself, open an issue. Issues are public, so don't paste the split's link there; we'll reply with a private way to identify it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact
